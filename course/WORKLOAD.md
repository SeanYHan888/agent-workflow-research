## Capacity and uncertainty

The normal **15h week** provides **13h directed work** (10 core/labs + 3 project) and **2h exploration**. Thus 342–543 directed hours imply approximately **27–42 active study weeks** before contingency. A provisional **20% revision/recovery allowance** gives 411–652 directed hours, or **32–51 active study weeks**, plus explicit breaks. These are planning ranges, not an agreed finish date. The allocation between core and project work can be adjusted explicitly within the total; a project-slot bottleneck can lengthen the calendar.

Conference leave contributes zero required hours and creates no catch-up debt. Optional conference notes may enter idea intake later; attendance is not automatically course assessment evidence. The old foundation project's last date is not graduation from the expanded program.

## Make projects small enough to execute

Keep one primary module and one bounded practical project. Milestone-hour estimates are not permission to put 45–75 hours into a three-week side project. At the usual three project hours/week, a practical slice can use at most nine hours; a primary teaching block has its separately allocated core/lab time.

Before activation, write a useful independent deliverable, entry evidence, estimated hours, acceptance test and owner. Split a larger section into separate project owners before scheduling it; preserve its tasks and evidence through the vault's structural-change workflow. The current Linux/coordination backlog particularly needs subdivision before activation. Calendar labels alone cannot make that backlog fit a 30-hour sprint.

Use rolling planning: commit the next two study weeks, use a third week only as an explicit bounded completion/review window, and keep distant dates provisional or unassigned. If an initial diagnostic shows the active scope cannot fit, split it at entry instead of repeatedly extending its deadline. Resource-completion work remains owned and visible when capability practice is split.

## Evidence needed at restart

The teacher has inspected the current project logs, not administered a new test. M1 is credited; M2 onward remain unassessed unless the learner supplies newer evidence. Begin with the [45-minute restart diagnostic](../teaching/RESTART-DIAGNOSTIC.md), inspect the actual Boot.dev checkpoint, and revise the remaining-hour estimate. No learner task is completed by preparing this forecast.

At each weekly review, record actual focused hours, one explanation/artifact and the smallest remaining gap in the owning project. At a milestone assessment, replace that row's forecast with demonstrated work plus a new estimate for unmet criteria. Changes to agreed deadlines still follow the charter.
