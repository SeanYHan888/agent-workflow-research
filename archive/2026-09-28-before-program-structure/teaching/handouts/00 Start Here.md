## How to study

Open the current handout, attempt the question, inspect the selected source, then explain it in your own words. We will review your explanation and choose the next small exercise. You do not need to read every linked repository or finish every subject before building anything.

Use the existing project's **Progress log** for your date, focused minutes, explanation, code/output link and remaining question. Complete its task only after the corresponding work is demonstrated. These reading notes contain no second set of checkboxes.

Personal annotations can be added directly below a reading. Longer study notes can live in this folder and be linked from the owning project. The teacher keeps original handouts and course preparation in the classroom; your annotations will be preserved when material is revised.
