# Course ideas and placement

This is the intake and decision index. Execution tasks, time and completion evidence belong in the linked Obsidian project once activated. The [charter](CHARTER.md) owns workload and change authority.

## Intake process

Capture the idea in the student's terms. Explain its connection to a milestone, its prerequisites, the smallest useful experiment, and its time source. Classify it as an example, equivalent exercise, project option, elective, proposed milestone, or deferred/out-of-scope idea. Every idea gets considered; capture does not promise implementation.

Use up to the normal two-hour exploration allowance for a first investigation. More work needs an explicit project/study allocation and displaced work. A useful equivalent exercise retains the original assessment. Required-scope changes or agreed deadline changes follow the charter's approval process. Review the exploration's result and integrate, defer with a revisit condition, or close with findings.

## Record template

Use a short row for a small idea. Create a brief only when needed for an experiment or significant choice:

- Stable ID, date and original idea.
- Learning/practical purpose and related milestone.
- Role, prerequisites and currently known constraints.
- Smallest useful experiment and evidence to collect.
- Timebox, source of hours and displaced work.
- Decision, rationale and approval link when required.
- Owning project after activation; otherwise no duplicate task list.
- Result and revisit condition. Keep the history when the disposition changes.
