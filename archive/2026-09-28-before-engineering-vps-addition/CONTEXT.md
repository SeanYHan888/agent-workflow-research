# Distributed AI Agent Systems

Shared language for organizing study and reviewing learning in this course.

## Language

**Classroom**:
The workspace containing the course plan, teaching preparation, original handouts and material references.
_Avoid_: A second progress tracker

**Reading room**:
The student's Obsidian course folder containing assigned readings, handouts and links to the project that owns each assignment.
_Avoid_: Another task backlog

**Section**:
A stage of the course with a concrete capability and a checkpoint required before dependent work.
_Avoid_: A whole resource to read cover to cover

**Session**:
A short learning encounter organized around one question, an attempt and feedback.
_Avoid_: A completed section

**Checkpoint**:
A demonstration that the learner can explain, modify or diagnose the section's mechanism independently.
_Avoid_: Material availability, generated solution, checkbox without evidence

**Workflow project**:
One of the terminal collaboration, GUI collaboration or team-with-human-review outcomes, each with its own practical evidence.
_Avoid_: A product name

**Material shelf**:
The selected resources with their assigned role and point of use in the course.
_Avoid_: A requirement to finish every linked resource

**Core curriculum**:
The essential topics the teacher maintains across changes to readings, projects and schedule.
_Avoid_: Every topic mentioned by the student

**Milestone**:
A teacher-defined learning destination assessed through a working artifact or experiment, the student's explanation, and an unfamiliar modification or debugging task. The student may request additional milestones.
_Avoid_: A calendar date alone, a newly collected resource

**Exploration idea**:
A student-proposed question, topic or project to consider for a place in the course.
_Avoid_: An automatically scheduled assignment, an automatically required milestone

**Program stage**:
One of the four broad steps toward distributed AI agent systems: foundations, reliable single-host systems, distributed systems, or integrated capstone. A stage contains multiple milestones and may span multiple execution projects.
_Avoid_: One indefinitely extended project

**Exploration allowance**:
The two hours within the weekly 15-hour course budget reserved for investigating student ideas; unused time returns to core work.
_Avoid_: Additional unbudgeted study time

**Course project**:
A bounded practical deliverable connected to a milestone or elective, with one owning execution note and an explicit allocation of course time.
_Avoid_: An indefinitely extended program, a duplicate progress checklist

**Elective**:
An optional study or project choice that uses the shared course budget without becoming a required graduation outcome.
_Avoid_: An unbudgeted extra requirement

**Change proposal**:
A concrete revision to required milestones, core outcomes or agreed milestone deadlines, including its impact on prerequisites, workload and existing learning evidence.
_Avoid_: Silently replacing the curriculum after a new idea
